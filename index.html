What it is

117 short lessons across 10 topics. Each lesson is a run of questions: you tap, drag, sort or type an answer and find out straight away whether it is right.

Every answer comes with a "Why?" that explains the physics. The diagrams are drawn from the physics itself, and in some lessons you move the parts yourself.

As you practise, Upthrust gives an estimate of your grade: an estimate from practice, never a prediction.

Who it is for

Students in England taking GCSE Physics or GCSE Combined Science, at Foundation or Higher tier.

Your exam board

When you start, you choose your exam board, your course and your tier. From then on, Upthrust shows only what is on your course, in your own board's words.
