Getting started

How do I choose my board, course and tier?

You choose them when you first open the app. You can change them later in Settings.

Can I change my tier or course later?

Yes, in Settings.

Can I stop halfway through a lesson?

Yes. It picks up where you left off.

Your progress

Where is my progress kept?

On your phone only.

I changed phone and my progress has gone

Your progress stays on the phone it was made on, because there is no account to move it with.

How do I start again?

In Settings, choose “Start again”. Your lessons and answers are wiped, and your course stays.

Why is there no grade band yet?

It appears once there is enough practice across enough days and topics. Home says what is still needed.

Can I set my own exam dates?

Yes, in Settings.

Privacy

What does the app collect?

Nothing. There is no account, and your answers and progress stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.

Appearance

Can I use dark mode?

Yes. It follows your phone, or you can choose light or dark in Settings.

Need more help?

Email support@upthrust.app and we will reply as soon as we can.
