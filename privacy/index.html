The app

The app keeps these things on your phone:

  • the exam board, course and tier you chose
  • your answers, when you gave them, and whether they were right
  • your progress through the lessons
  • if you set them: your exam dates, a target grade and how many sessions a week you plan
  • whether you chose the light or dark look

It keeps them so it can mark your answers, bring questions back on later days and estimate a grade band.

None of it is sent anywhere. The app has no account, no server and no analytics.

Deleting your data

In Settings, “Start again” wipes your lessons and answers. Your course stays.

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, there is nothing else to delete, and progress does not move to a new phone.

Reporting a problem

A “Report a problem” email option is planned but switched off. If it is switched on later, it will only open an email in your own mail app, with your course and the app version filled in, and nothing is sent unless you press Send yourself. This page will be updated first.

This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Everything on it comes from the site itself.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security and operation. You can read GitHub's own privacy statement.

Children

The app is made for students aged about 14 to 16. It asks for no personal information and has no way to contact anyone.
